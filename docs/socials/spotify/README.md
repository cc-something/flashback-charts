# Spotify Discovery Plan

## Role

Spotify is not the main click-through channel. Its job is to make `Flashback Charts` discoverable anywhere people are already searching for chart history, decade playlists, or nostalgic Australian hits.

Success on Spotify should be measured through:

- playlist followers
- playlist saves
- profile follows
- branded search lift for `Flashback Charts` and `flashbackcharts.com`
- occasional assisted traffic from profile and playlist descriptions

Use [`../SOCIAL_VOICE_GUIDE.md`](../SOCIAL_VOICE_GUIDE.md) for every playlist title, description, and promotional mention.

## Profile Setup

Profile name: `Flashback Charts Australia`

Short bio copy:

`Australia's chart archive from 1940 to today. Explore the year-by-year charts at flashbackcharts.com`

Profile image brief:

Use a simple square mark derived from the existing site identity. It should read clearly at tiny sizes, avoid extra text, and use one high-contrast background colour.

Header principle:

Keep decade playlists visually related to the site's decade aesthetics, but simplify them hard for square cover art. Small text and busy collage disappear in Spotify search.

## Playlist Naming Rules

1. Put the searchable phrase first.
2. Use `Australia` or `Australian` when the intent is national chart discovery.
3. Use `Chart Hits`, `Top Songs`, `#1 Hits`, `Decade`, and `Year-End` language naturally.
4. Keep `Flashback Charts` in the description even when it does not fit cleanly in the title.
5. Keep titles readable at a glance rather than stuffing in every keyword.

## Description Formula

Use this structure:

`Public playlist from Flashback Charts Australia. [What this playlist covers in plain language.] More year-by-year charts at flashbackcharts.com.`

Example:

`Public playlist from Flashback Charts Australia. Big songs from Australian charts across the 1980s. More year-by-year charts at flashbackcharts.com.`

## Publishing Cadence

- Tuesday `11:00 AM Australia/Melbourne`: refresh one featured playlist
- First Monday of each month: rotate the pinned or promoted playlist family
- Quarterly: review decade playlists for missing or unavailable tracks
- Whenever a Facebook or X.com post spikes around a year or decade, update the closest matching playlist within `24` hours

## Cross-Promotion Rules

- Mention Spotify once per week on Facebook, usually in a comment or end-of-week archive push
- Mention Spotify once per week on X.com, usually as a reply or follow-up rather than the main post
- Keep social copy focused on the site first; Spotify is the supporting discovery surface

## Operational Notes

- Keep every playlist public.
- Keep descriptions consistent so the brand name repeats naturally.
- Do not rely on Spotify users clicking out immediately.
- Treat Spotify as a searchable memory layer that makes it more likely people later search for `Flashback Charts` directly.

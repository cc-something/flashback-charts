# Flashback Charts Australia

Top 10 songs in Australia by year, from 1940 to present.

## Roadmap

- **Monetisation** — banner ads, pay/donate to remove ads, flat-fee mobile app with no ads.
- **Growth** — analytics, newsletter signup modal.
- **Trust & compliance** — privacy policy.
- **Quality** — confirm playback works on mobile.
- **Platform** — native mobile app (ad-free, flat fee).
